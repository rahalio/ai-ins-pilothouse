# Pilothouse — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Chief Innovation Officer

- As the Chief Innovation Officer, I want every bet mapped to the profit pool it defends, so that I can show the board we are spending where the book is threatened.
- As the Chief Innovation Officer, I want committed capital split by horizon, so that a long-dated bet is not killed for failing a near-term payback test.
- As the Chief Innovation Officer, I want realised ratio movement per bet, so that next year's envelope is argued from outcomes rather than from activity.
- As the Chief Innovation Officer, I want pilots past their stop date reported as breaches, so that the portfolio does not silently fill with undead experiments.

### Business-unit sponsor and underwriting actuary

- As a motor sponsor, I want to agree the measurement population and window before a telematics pilot launches, so that the result is not litigated after the fact.
- As an underwriting actuary, I want a pilot blocked from varying price until clearance is recorded, so that an experiment does not create a pricing-conduct exposure.
- As a business-unit sponsor, I want to see the run cost I would inherit at scale-up before I accept ownership, so that a central pilot does not become an unfunded liability in my expense base.
- As an underwriting actuary, I want the persistency effect measured alongside the loss-ratio effect, so that a discount-led pilot is not scored on claims alone.

### Corporate venture investment manager

- As an investment manager, I want financial return and strategic effect reported separately for each stake, so that our portfolio story is honest.
- As an investment manager, I want to see which of our portfolio companies are also running pilots with us, so that the investment thesis and the commercial relationship are managed as one position.
- As an investment manager, I want the data-sharing terms of any commercial pilot with an investee recorded, so that our shareholding does not become the reason we gave away our loss experience.

### Regulatory affairs and third-party risk manager

- As a regulatory affairs officer, I want to grant or withhold clearance per pilot per jurisdiction, covering pricing behaviour, licensing implications, and aviation or other sector authorisations, so that the perimeter is enforced by the system.
- As a regulatory affairs officer, I want peer-to-peer and pooling pilots flagged as licensing and capital questions rather than product experiments, so that we do not launch an unlicensed carrier.
- As a third-party risk manager, I want the partner's dependency classification and exit and data-return provisions recorded before data moves, so that we can unwind a failed partnership without stranding policyholder data.

### Claims, catastrophe, and workforce planning

- As a claims transformation lead, I want loss-adjustment pilots measured on cycle time and indemnity accuracy together, so that faster adjustment does not raise leakage.
- As a catastrophe manager, I want aerial and sensor-based response pilots to record their effect on exposure and accumulation views, so that our reinsurance arrangements reflect how we now respond.
- As a workforce planner, I want each bet's operating-model consequence recorded at admission, so that the consolidation implied by scaling is planned rather than announced.

### Executive committee and portfolio administrator

- As an executive committee member, I want one quarterly view of capital, pools, horizons, outcomes, and stops, so that innovation is governed like any other capital allocation.
- As a portfolio administrator, I want a bet's channel-conflict assessment recorded where intermediated distribution is affected, so that broker relationships are managed deliberately rather than discovered in a complaint.
